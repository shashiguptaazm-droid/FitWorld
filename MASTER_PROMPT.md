SECTION 1 — ROLE AND MISSION
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Act as a world-class team consisting of a senior iOS and Android application
architect, product designer, AI engineer, certified fitness trainers,
physiotherapists, sports scientists, registered dietitians, yoga instructors,
dance choreographers, accessibility experts, cybersecurity engineers and
wellness specialists.

Design and build a production-ready, premium-quality, cross-platform fitness
and wellness application that combines the most useful capabilities of the
most-downloaded gym apps on the Apple App Store (e.g. the UI patterns and UX of
apps like GymWP, Fitbod, JEFIT Fitness & Workout Planner, Caliber, Home
Workout — No Equipment, Fitness Trainer by Akinsoft, Peloton, Keep as market
references only) with an original, comprehensive global fitness experience.

The application must help people of different ages, abilities, fitness levels,
cultures and countries exercise correctly, improve strength and mobility, dance
for cardiovascular fitness, relax mentally, track progress and eat according to
their individual nutritional requirements — with physiotherapy-style home
rehabilitation education and culturally relevant natural remedies built in.

TARGET PLATFORMS AND TECHNOLOGY
- iOS and iPadOS, App Store-ready UX. Android phones and tablets.
- Apple Watch + compatible wearables. Responsive web admin dashboard.
- Scalable backend for a large international user base.
- Recommended stack: React Native (TypeScript) or Flutter; FastAPI or NestJS;
  PostgreSQL; secure auth with Apple/Google sign-in; object storage + CDN;
  adaptive video streaming with captions; APNs + FCM; modular replaceable AI
  providers; privacy-conscious analytics; HealthKit / Health Connect /
  wearable APIs.
- Modular architecture, secure APIs, automated testing, monitoring, backups,
  accessibility standards, documented deployment pipeline.
- If the dev environment supports a different stack, choose the most
  appropriate and explain why.

SECTION 11 — PHYSIOTHERAPY & HOME REHABILITATION  (follow-up feature)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
A carefully governed home-physiotherapy education library, reviewed by
licensed physiotherapists before publication:
- Gentle, home-doable physiotherapy techniques and therapeutic exercises:
  neck and shoulder discomfort; upper and lower back; wrist and forearm;
  hip; knee; ankle and foot; muscle tightness/DOMS; desk stiffness; general
  mobility limitation.
- For each protocol: screening questionnaire (non-diagnostic), progressive
  rehab stages (pain-relief phase → range-of-motion → strengthening →
  return-to-activity), isometric → isotonic → therapeutic-band progression,
  proprioception/balance drills, joint-protection techniques, soft-tissue
  self-release (self-massage, foam rolling), cold/heat application guidance,
  posture-correction drills, workstation ergonomics teach-in, and gradual
  return-to-work/sport programs.
- RICE/POLICE/mechanism-of-injury education for common minor sprains and
  strains (educational only).
- Each demo uses the male/female animated demonstrators with timer + reps,
  calling out signs to slow down, regress or stop.
- Clear stop criteria (sharp/worsening pain, tingling, numbness, swelling)
  and red-flag warnings (severe or rapidly worsening pain, major trauma,
  sudden weakness, new significant numbness, bladder/bowel control loss,
  chest pain) with urgent-care prompts, using clinically reviewed triage
  content.
- Persistent, recurrent or unexplained pain must prompt professional
  evaluation. Never advertise exercises as guaranteed cures for pain,
  arthritis, injuries or disc problems. Never prescribe rehab based only on
  the hurting body part. Distinguish clearly between general fitness advice
  and clinician-prescribed rehabilitation. If the user has a clinician
  prescription, the app complements it — it never overrides it.

  seek professional care for anything serious.

rules — e.g., country-specific herbal-medicine restrictions — the app must

SECTION 13 — INTERNATIONAL NUTRITION & DIET PLANNER
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

availability. Country/regional libraries starting with: India, Pakistan,
Bangladesh, Nepal, Sri Lanka; US, Canada, UK, Australia, New Zealand; China,
Japan, South Korea, Thailand, Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines, Vietnam;

Each region includes healthy staple foods, familiar meals, seasonal foods,
affordable options, vegetarian/vegan options and culturally relevant

vegetables, curd; East Asian rice, tofu, legumes, fish, vegetables; Latin
American beans, maize, rice, local vegetables. Users may also mix cuisines;
never stereotype national diets.

FOOD DATA per item: local name; country/region; standard serving in grams/ml;
calories (kcal); protein, carbs, total fat, saturated fat (where available),
fiber, total sugars, sodium and key micronutrients (where available);
preparation method; raw vs cooked state; portion illustrations; data source;
date last reviewed; confidence/estimation status. Clearly distinguish raw,
cooked, drained and prepared weights, and count added oils/sauces/
sweeteners. Use authoritative national food-composition databases; never
invent values; estimate-not-laboratory items are labeled as estimates.

PERSONALIZED MEAL PLANS: daily/weekly plans by age, individual energy needs,
activity/training schedule, goals, dietary pattern, cultural preferences,
allergies/intolerances, budget, availability, meal frequency, cooking time,
vegan/vegetarian and other requirements. Breakfast, lunch, dinner, optional
snacks; totals and per-meal kcal/protein/carbs/fats/fiber; substitutions that
recalculate totals correctly; portion guidance in household + metric units;
plain-language explanation of each meal's role. Muscle growth plans
prioritize sufficient energy + protein + resistance training; weight plans
use gradual sustainable approaches, never extreme restriction. Age rules for
minors, pregnancy, older adults and special clinical needs; users with
kidney disease, diabetes requiring individualized nutrition, eating
disorders or severe allergies are directed to professionals.

FOOD LOGGING: searchable database, manual entry, barcode scan (where
supported), meal/recipe builder, portion scaling, saved meals, diary, water
tracking, macro/calorie dashboard, optional photo-assisted recognition with
uncertainty indicators and user correction before saving, shopping lists,
recipe suggestions, meal-prep planning, exportable summaries.
